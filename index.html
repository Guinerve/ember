Bushfire spread on real terrain west of Canberra (the Brindabellas). Click the map to ignite, set the weather, and play the fire forward.

Hillshade from a ~62 m terrain grid. Wind arrow shows where the fire is pushed.

Educational model, not an operational prediction tool. No spotting, no fuel-moisture dynamics, no suppression, uniform fuel. Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM-derived), not yet a Geoscience Australia ELVIS tile.
